=== INVESTMENT STYLE GUIDE ===
LARGE CAP: Relatively safe, liquidity high, FII friendly
- Examples: Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank, Infosys, ICICI Bank

MID CAP: Higher growth potential, more volatile
- Strong sectors: Capital goods, specialty chemicals, specialty finance

SMALL CAP: High risk / high reward — for long-term investors only
- Avoid in uncertain rate environments

=== RISK FACTORS FOR INDIAN MARKET (FY26) ===
1. US recession risk could trigger FII outflows
2. Geopolitical tensions (India-Pakistan border, Middle East)
3. El Niño impact on monsoon — rural economy risk
4. RBI regulatory tightening on unsecured lending (personal loans, credit cards)
5. Global crude oil spike — inflation and CAD widening risk
