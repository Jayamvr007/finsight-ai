=== ANNUAL REPORT SUMMARY FY2024-25 ===

REVENUE & PROFITABILITY
- Total Revenue: $30.2 Billion USD (₹2,51,656 Crore) — up 5.6% YoY in USD terms
- EBIT Margin: 24.5% — maintained despite global slowdown (industry-leading)
- Net Profit (PAT): ₹48,553 Crore — flat growth due to wage hikes
- EPS: ₹132.75 per share
- Free Cash Flow: ₹50,874 Crore (FCF conversion: 104.8% of PAT — excellent)

- North America: 52.2% of revenue (largest market, grew 4.1%)

- India: 5.7% (grew 14.2% — fastest growing geography)
- Rest of World: 12.6%

SEGMENT REVENUE BY VERTICAL

- Life Sciences: 8.5%
- Technology & Services: 17.5%

KEY FINANCIAL METRICS
- Market Cap: ~₹14,20,000 Crore

- Debt: Almost zero — net cash positive company
- Return on Equity (ROE): 51.3% — exceptional capital efficiency
- Dividend Yield: 1.9% — ₹28 + special dividend ₹18 per share (total ₹46)
- Buyback history: ₹17,000 Crore buyback completed in FY24

DEAL WINS & PIPELINE
- Total Contract Value (TCV) of new deals: $42.7 Billion (FY25) — record high

- AI and Cloud deals: 68% of new deals include AI transformation component

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS (2025)
- TCS AI platform "WisdomNext" deployed at 200+ enterprise clients
- Headcount: 601,546 employees — attrition improved to 12.5% (from 17.8%)
- New AI labs opened in Bengaluru, Amsterdam, and Toronto
- Strong deal pipeline in Europe driven by cost optimization mandates

ANALYST CONSENSUS
- 22 out of 38 analysts: HOLD (8 BUY, 8 SELL)
- Average target price: ₹4,050 (current: ~₹3,820)
- Upside potential: ~6% — fairly valued at current levels

RISKS TO MONITOR
1. Demand slowdown in North America — US recession risk in 2025
2. BFSI client discretionary spending cuts
3. Rupee appreciation could hurt revenue in INR terms
4. AI disruption could reduce headcount-dependent billing model
5. Increasing competition from Infosys, HCLTech in large deal pursuit
