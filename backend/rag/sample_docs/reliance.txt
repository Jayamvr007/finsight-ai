=== ANNUAL REPORT SUMMARY FY2024-25 ===

REVENUE & PROFITABILITY
- Total Revenue: ₹10,09,652 Crore (FY25) vs ₹8,97,488 Crore (FY24) — 12.5% YoY growth
- EBITDA: ₹1,82,454 Crore — EBITDA margin at 18.1%
- Net Profit (PAT): ₹79,020 Crore — up 6.8% from previous year
- EPS: ₹116.8 per share

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
1. Jio Platforms (Telecom)
   - Subscribers: 489 million (largest telecom in India)
   - ARPU: ₹197.5/month — grew 13% YoY driven by tariff hike
   - 5G rollout completed across 10,000+ cities
   - Revenue: ₹1,27,416 Crore

2. Reliance Retail
   - Revenue: ₹3,26,024 Crore — fastest growing segment
   - Physical stores: 18,918 across India
   - Digital commerce integration via JioMart
   - Acquiring premium brands: Hamleys, Lotus, GAP India

3. Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C)
   - World's largest integrated refinery at Jamnagar
   - Refining capacity: 68.2 MMTPA
   - Revenue: ₹6,06,812 Crore
   - Headwind: Global crude volatility and falling petrochemical margins

KEY FINANCIAL METRICS
- Market Cap: ~₹19,20,000 Crore (₹19.2 Lakh Crore)
- P/E Ratio: 24.1x (sector average: 22x)
- Debt-to-Equity: 0.37 (healthy, reduced from 0.45 in FY23)
- Return on Equity (ROE): 9.2%
- Dividend Yield: 0.32% — ₹10 per share

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS (2025)
- New Energy business: ₹75,000 Crore investment in solar, green hydrogen
- Reliance Jio IPO expected in 2025–26 — potential unlocking of value
- Partnership with Disney+Hotstar for JioCinema content
- AI integration in Jio networks for predictive maintenance

ANALYST CONSENSUS
- 28 out of 34 analysts: BUY
- Average target price: ₹1,680 (current: ~₹1,420)
- Upside potential: ~18%
- Key risk: O2C margin compression, regulatory changes in telecom

RISKS TO MONITOR
1. Crude oil price volatility impacts O2C margins
2. Intensifying competition from Airtel in 5G premium segment
3. Retail segment EBITDA margins remain under pressure (8.5% vs 12% target)
4. Succession and governance as Mukesh Ambani transition planning begins
